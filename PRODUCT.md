# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Georgia Tech undergraduate, graduate, and online students who need a fast, trustworthy answer about courses, schedules, registration, academic dates, admissions, and institutional policy. They may be checking a deadline between classes or resolving a high-consequence question late at night, so the interface must keep the question and the supporting official evidence close together.

## Product Purpose

BuzzBot is a conversation-first Georgia Tech student assistant. It turns official Georgia Tech schedule data and documents into clear answers with citations, freshness context, and structured course information. Success means a student can ask a natural question, understand the answer without reading a raw database result, and verify the source before acting.

## Brand Personality

Warm, composed, and trustworthy. BuzzBot should feel human-centered and quietly memorable, with enough editorial character to feel intentional while remaining familiar as a chat product.

## Anti-references

- Cold AI research-lab aesthetics and synthetic-blue visual language.
- B2B SaaS dashboards, enterprise automation copy, and nested card grids.
- Robot, microphone, waveform, honeycomb, or other generic AI motifs.
- Overly cute, chibi, fan-service, or narrow subculture styling.
- Generic corporate stock imagery or visuals implying that people are being replaced.
- A direct visual copy of the Georgia Tech public website or the ChatGPT brand.

## Design Principles

1. Keep conversation primary. History, account controls, and institutional branding remain secondary.
2. Keep evidence visible. Citations, source authority, and data freshness are part of the answer rather than optional metadata.
3. Put one idea on each surface. The empty state contains a prompt, a compact composer, and likely questions, not a dashboard.
4. Use Georgia Tech identity with restraint. Navy, gold, typography, and warm institutional surfaces carry the identity without decoration.
5. Keep personalization out of the conversation canvas. Course context belongs in Personalization when that product scope is implemented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All actions must be keyboard reachable, focus must remain visible, body text contrast must be at least 4.5:1, large text contrast at least 3:1, and practical touch targets at least 44px. Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. English is the initial interface language, but Korean and Japanese content must preserve phrase boundaries using `word-break: keep-all` and must never be made to fit through arbitrary character breaks.
